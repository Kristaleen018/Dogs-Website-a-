Why we love dogs?

We all know that dogs deserve the title of best pet because anybody who has met one will instantly understand how much dogs are loved. As a loyal companion, dogs provide unwavering devotion, that is, through their much wanted belly rubs, snack pilfering nature and their super enthusiastic support/response for everything you do. Care Tips For Dog Owners

  1. Puppies must visit the vet often to get their check-ups and shots.
  2. Basic training so they are well behaved.
  3. Carry for frequent walks as exercise so they would be healthy and happy to see outside.
  4. As directed by the vet, only feed them with nutritious meals, NO snacks.
  5. For good hygiene, baths must be regular.
  6. Use oatmeal shampoo for dry skin and tea tree oil shampoo for shiny coat.
  7. Use coconut oil for breath and teeth.
